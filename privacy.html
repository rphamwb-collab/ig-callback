Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

"Ryan Drafts" is a personal tool used only by its owner to send the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok drafts.

  • What it uses: the owner's TikTok login token and basic account info (display name and account ID), only to confirm the right account and upload drafts.
  • Where it is stored: only on the owner's own computer, in the operating system's protected credential store. Nothing is stored on a server.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or sent anywhere except to TikTok itself.
  • Other people: the tool does not collect data about anyone else.
  • Removal: the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Apps) and delete the stored token from their computer.
